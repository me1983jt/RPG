# Maine: The Frozen Wasteland — design baseline
Chapter One: The Road to Harmony. Offline, mobile-friendly native Fallout 2 CE adaptation using the existing Maine mod; HTML is a prototype, not a replacement engine. Preserve existing mod geography, timeline, dialogue and maps where compatible. Final story starts in York, proceeds through alternative routes, and unlocks Harmony as a hub. All values/names in the prototype are provisional unless confirmed. Latest choices override earlier conflicting choices (preset appearance, NPC faction leaders, classic-leaning mixed interface, fresh new game, expansion deferred). This is a consolidated paraphrase of the 92 approved decisions, not a claim of implementation.

## Round 1 (20)
1. Living world: growth/decline, new settlements, changing faction territory.
2. Movable objects (not unrestricted construction).
3. Winter: snow accumulation, frozen waterways, blizzards.
4. Underground mines, settlements and research sites.
5. Earned traits, abilities and titles.
6. Books, recipes/blueprints, history.
7. Profession combinations and organizations rather than mandatory specialization trees.
8. Companions with skills, gear, personal quests, relationships and independent decisions.
9. Homestead grows into town.
10. Craft supplies and clothing; machine repairs separate.
11. Business management with optional automation.
12. Regional production, traveling merchants, shortages.
13. NPC jobs, homes and relationships; no required ambition simulation.
14. Rumors, player news, radio/newspapers/boards.
15. Handcrafted quests, random jobs, dynamic events.
16. NPC memories of conversations, promises and favors; shared opinions.
17. Electricity for buildings, networks, machinery and communication.
18. Journal for discoveries and rumors.
19. After the main story: exploration, management and new stories.
20. Identity: living post-apocalyptic Maine and deep RPG character development.

## Final skill checks / action modes (2)
1. Hybrid checks: SPECIAL, skill, profession, equipment, personality, NPC relationship, luck, companion assistance. Mastered tasks guaranteed; failures task-dependent.
2. Classic turn-based, real-time with pause, automatic resolution; change outside encounters. Share progression and difficulty.

## Other confirmed constraints
40 SPECIAL points (1–10 each); level cap 50; skill cap 200%; six backgrounds; 12 professions, up to three active, five ranks; original Fallout inventory, quest journal and transitions remain native. York three factions; Harmony central hub; multiple passage solutions; motorized bicycle, car, truck, snowmobile with fuel, condition, cargo and upgrades; rent/buy homes; clothing equipment visible; permanent winter; adjustable survival; offline Android; APK after native integration. No redistribution of proprietary game data.
